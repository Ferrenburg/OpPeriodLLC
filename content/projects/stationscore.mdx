Small and volunteer departments do the training. The hard part is having the records to show for it when the insurance rating evaluation comes around.

StationScore keeps a department's training and equipment records organized around what the evaluation looks at, so the chief isn't digging through binders and spreadsheets the week before.

It's in development now. If your department wants to hear when it's ready, get in touch.
